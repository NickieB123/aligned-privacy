1. What we collect

Your account

You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a user ID and an email address (this can be a private "Hide My Email" address). If you share it the first time you sign in, Apple also gives us your name. We use these only to keep you signed in, keep your data in your account, and greet you by name. See Your sign-in account and Your saved data for details.

Basic details

Your birth year and ZIP code. They're saved to your private account so we can show your ballot and the right questions, and to turn on Simple language by default for ages 16–21. We never ask for your street address, gender, race or party.

Your quiz answers

For each question, we save which of the two statements was closer to your view (or "somewhere in between"), and which values you marked as mattering most. These answers reflect your political opinions, so we treat them as sensitive:

  • They're stored in your private account, and only you can read them.
  • They're used only to show you your matches.
  • They're never sold, shared, used for ads, or used to build a profile of you.

If you tried an early test version, answers from the old agree/disagree quiz are no longer used for anything. They're removed when you delete your data, or when we remove all old answers (we'll say so here).

"In your words"

If you write it, we save it to your account and print it at the top of your ballot guide. It's never used for matching, and nobody reads or analyzes it.

Purchases

If you buy an Election Unlock or the Annual Pass, we record what you bought, which election it's for, and the App Store transaction ID, so your purchases work on all your devices. Apple handles payment; we never see your card.

Reports you choose to send

  • Report an error about a candidate: we save the candidate, the topic, your message, the source link you give, the app version, and your account ID (so we can limit spam). Staff read these to fix mistakes.
  • Flag this question (test versions only): anonymous. We save the question, which statement, the boxes you ticked, your optional note and the app version, but not who you are.

2. What we don't collect

  • No advertising or analytics trackers, and no crash-reporting tools.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising ID.
  • No precise location. The app never asks for your location; we only know the ZIP code you type.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

3. Your sign-in account (Rift)

When you sign in to Aligned with your Apple ID, we create an account for you on our sign-in service, Rift. It keeps only what it needs to recognize you next time.

What Rift keeps

  • An internal account number (ID) for you.
  • The ID Apple gives us for you.
  • Your email address, if Apple chooses to share it, and a note of whether it's one of Apple's private relay ("Hide My Email") addresses.
  • Your name, if Apple provides it.
  • The date you created your account, and the date you last signed in.
  • A record for each device you're signed in on. It holds:
    • a scrambled form of that device's session key (never a usable copy);
    • the IP address the session started from;
    • when the session expires.
  • One more item: the token Apple gives us so we can disconnect your Apple ID from Aligned later, if you ask. It's encrypted before it's saved, using a key that's kept separately from the database.

Because you sign in with Apple, we never receive, create or store a password for you.

Where and how long

  • All of this is stored on Amazon Web Services in the United States (Northern Virginia).
  • Your account is kept until you delete it. It doesn't expire on its own.
  • Device session records expire 30 days after they're created.
  • The short-lived tokens the app uses to make requests last 60 minutes.
  • Rift's server logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically.

When you delete your account

  • Deleting your account in the app is permanent and immediate.
  • Your account record and everything attached to it (your sessions, name, email address and the ID Apple gave us for you) is erased, not just switched off. We can't bring it back.
  • At the same time, we tell Apple to revoke the connection between your Apple ID and Aligned, so Aligned no longer appears in the list of apps using your Apple ID.

4. Your saved data (Supabase)

Everything you do in the app is saved in our database, run by Supabase, in the United States (Northern Virginia). It's linked to your account ID, so it follows you to a new phone.

What's stored there

  • Your quiz answers, and the values you marked as mattering most (plus any old answers from early test versions).
  • "In your words", if you wrote it.
  • Your ZIP code and birth year.
  • Your settings and name: the Simple language setting, and the name shown in the app.
  • Purchase records: which elections you unlocked, Annual Pass bonuses, and App Store transaction IDs.
  • Error reports you send about candidates.
  • While a deletion is in progress, a short note saying how far it got (see below).

The database only lets each person read their own account. Our staff can reach it for running and fixing the service.

Delete my data

  • Settings → Delete my data erases everything listed above, along with your Rift account.
  • If something interrupts the deletion (for example, you lose signal), the app tells you and lets you try again.
  • Daily safety net: once a day, our server checks for accounts whose sign-in account was closed but whose saved data wasn't fully deleted, and deletes that data. It only ever acts on accounts that were closed.

5. How we use your information

  • To run the app: sign you in, save your answers, show your ballot and your matches, and make your ballot guide.
  • To make your purchases work on all your devices.
  • To fix mistakes you report, and to make the questions fairer.
  • To keep the service safe and working (for example, limiting spam).

We don't use your information for ads, and we don't sell it.

7. Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete my data permanently deletes your sign-in account and everything we keep about you, on our servers and your phone. That includes old quiz answers and your reports.
  • It's permanent and immediate. Details: sign-in account and saved data, including the daily safety net.
  • Anonymous flags can't be tied to you, so there's nothing of yours to delete there.
  • If you have an Annual Pass, also cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple keeps its own record of your purchases.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account and saved data: until you delete them.
  • Sign-in device sessions: 30 days. Request tokens: 60 minutes. Rift server logs (with IP addresses): 90 days.
  • Error reports: until you delete your data. We may keep a record of the correction we made to a candidate's page, without your details.

9. Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each person read their own account, and staff access is limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Candidate information

  • Candidate details and positions come from public records (for example the Federal Election Commission, Congress.gov, the U.S. Senate and House, Open States, and state and county election offices), from candidates' public statements and campaign sites, and from candidates' own answers to our questionnaire.
  • Every position links to its source, and two reviewers check each one before it appears.
  • For campaign money we show only totals from public filings. We never show or store individual donors' names or addresses.
  • Candidates who answer our questionnaire are confirmed through official filing records. We keep their answers, their optional statements, and the contact details from their official filing that we used to confirm them.

11. Children

You must be at least 13 to use Aligned. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your answers and basic details any time in the app.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Delete my data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under state privacy laws such as those in California and Texas), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information. Contact us and we'll help. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

13. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, we'll tell you in the app.
