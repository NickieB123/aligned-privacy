Delete your data

  1. Open Aligned and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your Aligned account and everything we keep about you, right away: your quiz answers, "In your words", ZIP code, birth year, settings and purchase records. It also disconnects your Apple ID from Aligned. It can't be undone.

If it says it couldn't finish (for example, you lost signal), tap Try again. If you can't open the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll help.

Deleting your data doesn't cancel an Annual Pass. Cancel it with Apple too (below).

Cancel the Annual Pass

Apple handles subscriptions, so you cancel in your iPhone's settings, not in the app:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Aligned, then Cancel Subscription.

You keep the Annual Pass until the end of the year you paid for. The presidential bonus you earned stays yours.

Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Bought something but don't see it?

In Aligned, go to Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If an Election Unlock still doesn't show, email us with the date of purchase.

Something about a candidate looks wrong?

Open the candidate and tap Report an error. Add a link to a source if you have one. Every position in Aligned needs a source, and two reviewers check each one.
